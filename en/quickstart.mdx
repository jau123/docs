## Generate your first image

1. Enter a prompt or choose **Use Idea** on a gallery image that includes one.
2. Select a model and check the price on **Generate**. Attach a reference image if you want to guide the subject or style.
3. Select **Generate** and open the result in your history.

Daily free credits apply to basic models. See [model pricing](/en/features/models) for the options available to your account.
